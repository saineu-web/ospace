Set-up guide

Four steps and you're live

1

Download & create your account

Install the app, then sign up using your phone number as the username and your date of birth as the password.

2

Complete your profile

Fill in your personal details and set the days and times you're available for rides.

3

Enter the service number

When asked, enter service number {{ SITE.service_number }} to link your account to Ospace.

4

Sign in & go

Sign in with your credentials. Once your Ospace application is approved, rides near you start appearing.
