Where does Ospace operate?

We are headquartered in Houston, Texas and serve Houston and the surrounding areas. Rides are matched to the driver closest to the student's home.

How do I upload my documents?

After applying you'll get access to the driver portal. Upload your license, insurance, registration, photos and test results there, e-sign the driver agreements and submit for review. You can track the status of every item.

Which app do I use?

Ospace rides are dispatched through the ADROIT Driver app for iPhone and Android. Enter service number {{ SITE.service_number }} when you set up your account. See the set-up guide.

I'm a parent. How do I book rides?

Use the ride request form or call us. We'll confirm availability, pricing and your driver's details before the first ride.
