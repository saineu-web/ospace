About

All about us

We're Ospace, a company that believes transportation should be safe, reliable, and built around people's needs.

Connecting families, schools and communities with drivers they can trust

Ospace is a student transportation service headquartered in Houston, Texas. We match students with dependable local drivers for the school commute, and give independent drivers a flexible way to earn using their own vehicle.

For parents, that means peace of mind: a vetted driver, an in-person hand-over at both ends, and the same familiar face on the school run. For drivers, it means real flexibility: choose your days, choose your windows, and get paid per ride.

Great service begins with great people and real experience. That is why we are selective about who drives with us, and why we aim to exceed expectations on every ride across the Houston area.
