In the ride

We give you the ride that is near to you

Ospace assigns rides to the closest available driver, so drivers spend less time on the road between jobs and families get someone from their own neighbourhood.

Hand-over protocol

The parent or the teacher takes the kids from and to the car

Drivers never enter a home or a school building. At every pick-up and drop-off, an adult you know walks the student to or from the vehicle and the driver confirms the hand-over in the app.

  • Location — the driver waits at the agreed pick-up spot
  • Hand-over — parent, guardian or teacher brings the student to the car
  • Seat belts on — every student, every ride, no exceptions
  • Arrival — the student is released only to the designated adult
A typical school day

Morning run, afternoon run

  1. 1

    Ride offered in the app

    The driver closest to the student's home sees the ride first, with the pick-up time, address and school.

  2. 2

    Pick-up at home

    The driver arrives a few minutes early. The parent walks the student to the car and the driver taps "picked up".

  3. 3

    Drop-off at school

    A teacher or school staff member receives the student at the designated spot. Driver taps "dropped off" and the family is notified.

  4. 4

    Afternoon, in reverse

    Same driver where possible, same hand-over at both ends, home safe.
