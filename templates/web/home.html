Our services

Simple rides, done safely

Ospace matches students with dependable drivers for the school commute. Every ride follows the same four steps.

Pick-up

Collect students from home. A parent or guardian brings them to the vehicle.

Drive

Use your own vehicle. Any clean four-door car from the last 15 years qualifies.

Drop-off

Deliver students to school and back home, handed to a teacher or parent every time.

Earn money

A minimum of $50 for every two rides, paid per completed ride.
